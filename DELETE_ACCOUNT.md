# Delete your Robia data

**Effective date:** 01/11/2026

Robia is a private digital wardrobe app published by GusanitoLabs.

Robia does not create or maintain a separate Robia user account. You can use
the app without signing in. Connecting a Google account is optional and is
used only to back up your wardrobe to the private Robia app-data area of your
own Google Drive account.

## Delete your cloud backup

If you connected Google Drive, you can delete the Robia backup yourself:

- In Robia: Settings › Google Drive › “Delete backup from Google Drive” (removes
  photos, wardrobe data and sync metadata from the private app-data area), then
  “Disconnect”.
- Without the app: Google Drive on the web › Settings › Manage apps › Robia ›
  Options › “Delete hidden app data”, then remove Robia’s access at
  myaccount.google.com/permissions.
- Questions: email the.gusanito.lab@gmail.com with the subject line “Robia data
  deletion request”. We will guide you, as we hold no copy of your data.

## Data stored on your device

Data stored only on your device is under your control. You can delete wardrobe
items in Robia, clear the app’s storage in Android settings, or uninstall the
app. A remote deletion request cannot remove data that remains only on your
device.
