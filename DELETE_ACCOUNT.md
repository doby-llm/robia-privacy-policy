# Delete your Robia data

Robia is a private digital wardrobe app published by GusanitoLabs.

Robia does not create or maintain a separate Robia user account. You can use
the app without signing in. Connecting a Google account is optional and is
used only to back up your wardrobe to the private Robia app-data area of your
own Google Drive account.

## Request deletion of your cloud backup and associated data

If you connected Google Drive and want us to delete the Robia backup associated
with your Google account, email the.gusanito.lab@gmail.com with the subject line:

“Robia data deletion request”

Please send the request from the Google account used to enable Robia backup,
or include that Google account email address so that we can identify the
correct backup.

## Data that will be deleted

After we verify your request, we will delete the Robia backup stored in the
private app-data area of the Google Drive account you authorized. This includes:

- garment photographs;
- garment names, notes, tags, colours, categories and other wardrobe metadata;
- backup and sync metadata associated with that wardrobe backup.

Robia does not operate a server that stores your wardrobe data or create a
separate Robia account.

## Data stored on your device

Data stored only on your device is under your control. You can delete wardrobe
items in Robia, clear the app’s storage in Android settings, or uninstall the
app. A remote deletion request cannot remove data that remains only on your
device.

## Retention period

We process verified deletion requests within 30 days. We do not retain a copy
of the deleted cloud backup after deletion, except for the minimum information
necessary to document and handle the deletion request where legally required.

## Contact
