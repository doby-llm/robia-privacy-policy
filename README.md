# Privacy Policy for Robia

**Effective date:** 10/08/2026

Robia is a digital wardrobe app published by GusanitoLabs (“we”, “us”). This policy explains how Robia handles information when you use the app.

## Information you add to Robia

Robia stores the wardrobe information that you choose to add, including photographs of garments and optional details such as garment names, notes, colors, tags, categories, seasons, occasions, locations, and fit preferences. This information is used only to provide the wardrobe features you request.

Robia processes garment images on your device to support photo editing, background processing and garment-related information. We do not operate a server that receives your wardrobe data for these on-device features.

## Optional Google Drive backup

Google Drive backup is optional. If you choose to connect Google Drive and enable backup, Robia asks for authorization limited to its app data space (`drive.appdata`). Robia stores your wardrobe backup, including garment photographs and associated wardrobe metadata, in the private app data area of the Google account that you authorize. This area is not visible as a normal folder in Google Drive.

You can choose not to enable backup. When backup is not enabled, Robia keeps your wardrobe data on your device. You can disconnect Google Drive or delete the Robia backup through the app when that feature is available. Google’s handling of data is governed by Google’s own privacy policy.

## Sharing, advertising and sale of data

We do not sell your wardrobe data. Robia does not display third-party advertising and does not use your wardrobe information for behavioural advertising. We do not share your wardrobe data with third parties other than the optional storage of the backup in the Google Drive account that you choose to authorize.

## Data retention and deletion

Data stored only on your device remains there until you delete it, uninstall the app, or clear its app data. Optional Drive backup remains in the private Robia app data space until you delete the backup, revoke access, or otherwise remove it through the available controls. Device and cloud deletion may occur at different times while an offline change is waiting to sync.

## Security

Robia uses the security controls of your device and, when you opt in, the Google Drive account you authorize. No security method can guarantee absolute security; keep your device and Google account protected.

## Children

Robia is not directed to children. The app is intended for adults and is not designed for use by children.

## Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when Robia’s data practices change. We will update the effective date and, where appropriate, provide notice in the app or on this page.
